# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Friends playing casually together. Shared laughs come first, inclusion second,
and competition third.

## Product Purpose

**Mission:** Make friends laugh together through fast, messy drawings.

**Vision:** A drawing game friends can start from one link, on phones or laptops,
together or apart.

## Operating Context

Both settings matter: friends together in one room, mostly on phones, and remote
friends on a voice call, using a mix of phones and laptops.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The game already has room links, timed drawing, guessing, scores and round results.
The current layout is built for desktop. Comfortable portrait play, a shared
reveal wall and a downloadable game-night sheet are future work, specified in the
[roadmap](docs/roadmap.md).

## Product Principles

- Make frantic drawing build toward a funny round reveal.
- Let friends join and play with the devices they have.
- Celebrate messy drawings and funny guesses; keep scores easy to understand.
- Keep joining, controls and the next action clear during the rush.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The portrait-play target includes the guessing keyboard open, without requiring
rotation. Prompts and controls must remain readable and usable under time pressure.
Motion must respect reduced-motion preferences. The [accessibility statement](docs/accessibility.md)
records current coverage and limitations; these targets do not imply they are met.
